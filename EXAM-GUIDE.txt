--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FLOW FOR TEACHERS (BEFORE & DURING EXAM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Create Form] --> [Upload & Share App] --> [Exam Monitoring] --> [Teacher Override (if needed)]

STEP 1: PREPARE THE EXAM FORM
  - Create or open your quiz in Google Forms.
  - Recommended settings: Limit to 1 response, collect email addresses.

STEP 2: DISTRIBUTE THE APPLICATION TO STUDENTS
  - File to upload:
      Exam Lockdown-Portable.exe (located in the project "dist" folder)
  - Upload options:
      * Google Drive (Share -> Anyone with the link can view/download)
      * Google Classroom (Attach as assignment or material)
      * School Portal, OneDrive, or Dropbox
  - Share the download link with students before exam day.

STEP 3: TEACHER EMERGENCY EXIT (OVERRIDE)
  - If a student needs to exit before finishing:
      1. Go to the student's computer.
      2. Press Ctrl + Shift + X (or Cmd + Shift + X on Mac).
      3. Enter the teacher password: Teacher123!
      4. Click Unlock or press Enter. The app will immediately close.
  - Security note:
      * There is a 5-attempt limit with a 30-second lockout to prevent
        unauthorized guessing.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FLOW FOR STUDENTS (TAKING THE EXAM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Download App] --> [Double-Click .exe] --> [Answer in Kiosk] --> [Submit Form] --> [App Auto-Closes]

STEP 1: DOWNLOAD
  - Open the link sent by your teacher.
  - Download "Exam Lockdown-Portable.exe".
  - No installation, setup, or extra software is needed.

STEP 2: LAUNCH
  - Double-click "Exam Lockdown-Portable.exe".
  - First-time note: If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen:
      Click "More info" -> then click "Run anyway".

STEP 3: TAKE THE EXAM
  - The application opens in True Fullscreen Kiosk Mode:
      * Taskbar, desktop, and window borders are completely hidden.
      * Alt+Tab, Windows Key, F-keys, and dev tools are blocked.
      * External websites and popups are blocked.
      * Answer typing shortcuts work normally (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+A).
  - If Wi-Fi momentarily drops:
      * A dark connecting screen appears and retries every 5 seconds.
      * You will not lose your progress once reconnected.

STEP 4: SUBMIT & AUTO-EXIT
  - Answer all questions.
  - Click "Submit" at the bottom of the Google Form.
  - The confirmation page displays ("Your response has been recorded").
  - The lockdown banner appears:
      "Exam Submitted! Closing in 4 seconds..."
  - The app automatically closes itself and returns you to your desktop.
